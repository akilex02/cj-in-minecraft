# CJ in Minecraft v0.2.0

The first public release: Carl Johnson from GTA San Andreas plays Minecraft, with GTA running beside it.

- CJ from GTA San Andreas as the Minecraft player, with GTA's animations, and his clothes and body from your save.
- GTA's weapons with ammo, recipes, durability, repair and chest loot; twin pistols at top skill; fists and the baseball bat.
- Crouching, swimming and climbing as in GTA.
- The Greenwood (G key) with radio, headlights and fuel; the jetpack with its fuel.
- First person: CJ's arms and weapon from his eyes, and driving from the driver's seat.
- The installer `cj_setup.py`: `install`, `dress`, `icons`, `play`.
- Optional skating with the Skate 3 Rust engine, for Linux and Windows.

## What to download

- **`cj-mod-0.2.0.zip`** — everyone: the mod, the GTA bridge and the installer. Follow [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/akilex02/cj-in-minecraft/blob/main/INSTALL.md).
- **`cj-skate-0.2.0-linux.zip`** or **`cj-skate-0.2.0-windows.zip`** — optional, for skating with your own Skate 3. Follow
  [INSTALL-skate.md](https://github.com/akilex02/cj-in-minecraft/blob/main/INSTALL-skate.md) (the Windows pack's own guide is in Spanish).
- **`SHA256SUMS.txt`** — the checksums of the three packages.

Nothing of GTA San Andreas or Skate 3 is included: you need your own copies of the games.
