# Changelog

## v0.2.0

The first public release.

- CJ from GTA San Andreas as the Minecraft player, with GTA's animations, and his clothes and body from your save (`cj_setup.py dress`).
- GTA's weapons with ammo, recipes, durability, repair and chest loot; twin pistols at top skill; fists and the baseball bat.
- Crouching, swimming and climbing as in GTA.
- The Greenwood (G key) with radio, headlights and fuel; the jetpack with its fuel.
- First person: CJ's arms and weapon from his eyes, and driving from the driver's seat.
- The installer `cj_setup.py`: `install` (checks GTA, copies the bridge and the car's floor, converts the models, puts the mod in `mods`),
  `dress`, `icons` (a resource pack with your weapon icons), `play`.
- Optional skating with the Skate 3 Rust engine: a skate pack for Linux and one for Windows, `cj_setup.py skate` and `play-skate`.
